# Preliminary brand clearance: MALBIT · 말빛

Research date: 2026-08-10

This memo records a preliminary exact-name search for product-design purposes. It is not a legal opinion, a freedom-to-operate conclusion, or a substitute for a Korean trademark attorney's similarity search.

## Release recommendation

- Use **MALBIT · 말빛** as the distinctive product brand.
- Use `TOPIK` only in secondary, factual copy such as `TOPIK I·II prep` or `practice for TOPIK I and II`.
- Prefer an App Store title such as **MALBIT: Korean Learning**. Put the exam reference in the subtitle or description, not in the dominant logo.
- Always state that the app is independent, unofficial, and not affiliated with the exam administrator.
- Do not use the official TOPIK logo, seal, typography, screenshots, or wording that suggests authorization, certification, partnership, or official status.
- Keep the legacy `topik-quest` repository and Pages slug only for technical compatibility; do not treat it as customer-facing branding.

## TOPIK search findings in Korea

An exact `TOPIK` search in the official KIPRIS trademark database returned 38 records on the research date. The most relevant live records include:

| 한국어능력시험 (TOPIK) | 대한민국 (교육부 장관) | 41 | 41-2004-0027756 | 41-0125926-0000 | Korean-language testing, education, courses, online publications, academies |
| 한국어능력시험 (TOPIK) | 대한민국 (교육부 장관) | 16 | 40-2004-0058245 | 40-0644656-0000 | Examination papers, books, workbooks, and printed materials |

NIIED's official site identifies TOPIK as the Test of Proficiency in Korean administered by the National Institute for International Education under the Ministry of Education.

The practical conclusion is not that the word can never appear. Korea's Trademark Act Article 90(1)(2) limits a registered mark's effect where matters such as a product's purpose or method of use are displayed in an ordinary descriptive manner. Whether a specific title, logo, subtitle, keyword, or screenshot qualifies depends on its complete presentation and facts. Using `TOPIK` as this app's dominant source-identifying title therefore creates avoidable trademark, affiliation, and store-review risk in the same education field. Factual exam-preparation wording is safer when it is subordinate to a distinctive house mark and accompanied by a clear disclaimer.

## MALBIT / 말빛 preliminary findings

- Exact KIPRIS search for `MALBIT`: eight results were returned, all for the different term `MALBITT`; the located records were old or extinguished and did not reveal an exact live `MALBIT` registration in the most relevant software/education classes.
- Exact KIPRIS search for `말빛`: one active result, `말빛언어발달센터`, was located in class 44. No exact class 9 or 41 match was found in this limited search.
- Exact searches for `WORDLIGHT` and `말빛 원정` returned no KIPRIS records.
- Apple's official iTunes Search API returned no app result for exact `malbit` queries in the Korean, Japanese, or United States storefronts on the research date.

These are encouraging screening results, not clearance. Trademark risk also includes similar sound, appearance, meaning, transliteration, related goods/services, unregistered rights, pending records, and foreign rights. A professional search should therefore cover at least Korean classes 9, 16, 41, and 42, plus intended launch countries, before a paid commercial release.

## Final pre-release checks

1. Commission a similarity and transliteration search for `MALBIT`, `말빛`, and the proposed icon/logo in every launch territory.
2. Search pending as well as registered marks in the relevant goods and service classes.
3. Review the exact App Store title, subtitle, keywords, icon, screenshots, and disclaimer as one package.
4. Consider filing the selected mark before a public marketing push.
5. Preserve dated design, code, question-authoring, and asset-source records.
