# Grammar content review checkpoint — 2026-10-19

## Coverage honesty
The course currently authors a broad modern-standard core. A lesson is a pedagogical unit, not necessarily one official grammar entry: some bundle variants, some split senses. Do not describe 208 lessons as all Korean grammar, all 336 official reference entries, certified TOPIK coverage, or guaranteed proficiency. The official 336-entry reference has not been exhaustively cross-walked form-by-form and sense-by-sense.

The separate 12-stage comprehensive grammar table of contents is a broader learning roadmap, not a claim that every listed domain already has a functional app lesson. Every app count must come from loaded data, not the roadmap inventory.

## Assessment limits
These activities establish recognition, targeted formation, contextual selection and later retrieval. They do not independently prove open-ended paragraph writing, conversational competence or mastery of every polysemous use. A later review must remain later; showing the answer and immediately repeating it must not count as independent recall. Handwriting is a distinct skill and must not gate grammar progress.
