## Limits

Direct quotation coverage is intentionally narrower than the whole bank; inference, pictures, paraphrases without an exact supported quote and unverified legacy listening cues are not highlighted. The local vocabulary dictionary is finite; unknown conjugations need learner review/editing. Physical-device/touch and human auditory quality review are not claimed. Existing historical positive timing values are preserved. A saved unfinished attempt from an older build with no start timestamp begins measurement when rendered; its earlier elapsed time cannot be recovered. Physical-device and human auditory review remain separate from automated release verification.
