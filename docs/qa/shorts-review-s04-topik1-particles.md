## Reviewed learning contract

Each card asks one quick noun-role judgment:

- `에`: the destination reached by movement
- `에서`: the place where an action happens
- `(으)로`: the means or method used for an action
- `에게`: the person receiving or targeted by an action

The examples make the role decisive through `가다`, `공부하다`, travel by bus, or giving a gift to a
friend. Selected-choice feedback explains the chosen trap before optional full coaching compares all four
and gives one reusable verb-plus-noun-role method. Fixed choices are shuffled and restored through the
existing Shorts record.
