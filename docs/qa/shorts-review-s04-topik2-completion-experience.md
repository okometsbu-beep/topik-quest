## Reviewed learning contract

Each card asks one quick post-action judgment:

- `-아/어 버리다`: finish an action completely, sometimes with contextual regret or relief
- `-(으)ㄴ 끝에`: reach a final result after a long or repeated process
- `-아/어 본 적이 있다`: have past experience of doing the action
- `-아/어 놓다`: complete preparation in advance and keep the resulting state

The examples make the distinction decisive through `다`, `여러 번`, an explicit past experience, or preparation
before guests arrive. Selected-choice feedback explains the chosen trap before optional full coaching compares all
four and gives one reusable post-action method. Fixed choices are shuffled and restored through the existing Shorts record.
