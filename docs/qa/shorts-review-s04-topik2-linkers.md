## Reviewed learning contract

Each card asks one sentence-relation judgment on one Korean connective adverb:

- `따라서`: a result follows from the preceding cause or reason
- `반면에`: two different sides are contrasted
- `게다가`: another point is added in the same direction
- `다만`: the preceding statement remains true but gains a limitation or condition

The four relations do not overlap in the reviewed examples. Each selected option explains its own relation
before the optional full explanation maps all four and gives a reusable relation-first method. Fixed reviewed
choices are shuffled per card and restored from the existing Shorts record.
